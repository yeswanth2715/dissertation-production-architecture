# Dissertation Benchmark Scorecard

Use this scorecard to target high-level dissertation grading based on the BSBI/UCA Level 7 mark descriptors.

## Target

```text
Default target: 80-89 high distinction
Stretch target: 90-100 exceptional
Minimum final-ready target: 70-79 distinction-ready
```

## Final-Ready Gate

- [ ] No core academic dimension is below 70-level quality.
- [ ] Literature review is critical and thematic, not source-by-source description.
- [ ] Methodology is justified and rejected alternatives are addressed where relevant.
- [ ] Findings are not overstated.
- [ ] Analysis explains what the findings mean.
- [ ] Discussion connects findings with literature, theory, and research questions.
- [ ] Conclusion answers the whole dissertation question.
- [ ] Harvard referencing is consistent.
- [ ] No unresolved originality or plagiarism-risk issue remains.

## Survey Readiness Gate

- [ ] Introduction exists.
- [ ] Chapter One - Literature Review I exists.
- [ ] Chapter Two - Literature Review II exists.
- [ ] Chapter Three - Methodology exists.
- [ ] Research aim, objectives, and questions are clear.
- [ ] Survey design is justified by the methodology.
- [ ] Target participants and sampling logic are defined.
- [ ] Questionnaire items map to research objectives.
- [ ] Ethics and consent language are included where needed.
