# Originality And Non-Plagiarism Policy

Each topic must be researched and written independently.

## Required Rules

```text
- Do not reuse dissertation prose across topics.
- Do not reuse literature-review paragraphs from a previous project.
- Do not lightly paraphrase source material.
- Do not generate unsupported claims.
- Do not fabricate citations, pages, data, participants, findings, or sources.
- Use direct quotations only when necessary and cite them correctly.
- Keep source notes separate from final prose.
```

## Claim Discipline

Every material claim should map to one of:

```text
- a cited academic source
- a cited report or data source
- collected primary data
- a clearly marked project assumption
- the author's analysis derived from cited evidence
```

The `citation-claim-map.md` file is the audit trail for this mapping.

## Image Policy

Final dissertation images must be source-based, not AI-generated.

Visual styling and figure design must follow `architecture/visual-style-policy.md`.

Allowed:

```text
- figures from academic articles with correct source attribution
- charts generated from actual survey/interview/data findings
- screenshots or public-source images with correct referencing
- article/report-style images that are relevant to the argument
```

Not allowed:

```text
- AI-generated illustrations presented as evidence
- decorative images without academic purpose
- unsourced images
- copied images without figure captions and list-of-illustrations entries
```
