# Output Policy

Outputs are generated from the topic knowledge base and approved source materials.

Before output generation, the system must inspect the topic project and identify:

```text
- available source files
- missing inputs
- research design type
- pre-survey package status
- methodology status
- survey questionnaire status
- returned survey results status
- professor feedback status
- outputs already generated
```

## Required Output Types

```text
dissertation.docx
professor-feedback.docx
professor-feedback.md
survey-questionnaire.docx
survey-questionnaire.md
```

## Research Design Types

```text
survey-only: primary data comes mainly from questionnaire responses
mixed-methods: survey data is combined with interviews, case-study evidence, secondary data, or another method
secondary/case-study: no survey unless methodology explicitly adds one
conceptual: no survey unless methodology explicitly adds empirical collection
```

## Pre-Survey Package Gate

Survey questionnaire outputs must be generated only after the dissertation has been drafted and approved up to and including Chapter Three - Methodology, following the BSBI/UCA guide and template.

Required before questionnaire generation:

```text
- Introduction draft
- Chapter One - Literature Review I draft
- Chapter Two - Literature Review II draft
- Chapter Three - Methodology draft
- research aim
- research objectives
- research questions
- approved pre-survey package direction
- target participant group
- sampling logic
- ethics/consent requirements
- data analysis plan
```

After questionnaire generation, survey collection is manual. The questionnaire is assigned to the respective project participants outside the repo. Returned survey results are then added to the same topic project and used to generate findings, analysis, discussion, and final dissertation outputs.

## Dissertation Structure

The dissertation must follow this sequence:

```text
Title page
Declaration / academic ethics statement
Abstract
Acknowledgements
Table of Contents
List of Tables / Figures / Illustrations, if needed
Table of Abbreviations, if needed
Introduction
Chapter One - Literature Review I
Chapter Two - Literature Review II
Chapter Three - Methodology
Chapter Four - Findings / Analysis / Discussion
  4.1 Findings
  4.2 Analysis
  4.3 Discussion
Concluding Remarks
Bibliography
Appendix / Appendices
```

## Word Targets

```text
Abstract: around 200 words
Introduction: around 1,000 words
Chapter One: around 3,000 words
Chapter Two: around 3,000 words
Chapter Three: around 2,000 words
Chapter Four: around 5,000 words
Concluding Remarks: around 1,000 words
Total counted body: maximum 15,000 words
```

Excluded from word count:

```text
cover pages
abstract
contents
bibliography
appendices
```

## Formatting Rules

```text
Font: Times New Roman
Font size: 12
Alignment: justified
Line spacing: 1.5
Each chapter starts on a new page
Table of contents includes page numbers
Only chapters are numbered at the main chapter level
```

## Context-Based Subheading Rules

Subheadings must describe the actual content of the section.

Correct:

```text
1.1 Evolution of Agile Project Management in SMEs
1.2 Barriers to Agile Adoption in Remote Teams
2.1 Empirical Evidence on Agile Delivery Performance
3.1 Research Philosophy and Methodological Rationale
4.2.1 Interpretation of Survey Findings
```

Incorrect:

```text
1.1 Introduction
1.2 Background
2.1 Literature Review
3.1 Methodology
```

Numbering must match the chapter:

```text
Chapter One -> 1.x
Chapter Two -> 2.x
Chapter Three -> 3.x
Chapter Four -> 4.x
```
