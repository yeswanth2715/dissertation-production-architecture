# Extracted BSBI/UCA Rules

This file captures the current working rules extracted from the provided dissertation guide, Harvard referencing guide, and dissertation template.

## Dissertation Length

```text
Maximum word count: 15,000 words
Counted range: first word of Introduction to last word of Conclusion / Concluding Remarks
Excluded: cover pages, abstract, contents, bibliography, appendices
```

## Formatting

```text
Font: Times New Roman
Size: 12
Alignment: justified
Line spacing: 1.5
Every chapter starts on a new page
Table of contents includes page numbers
List of tables/figures/images appears after contents when needed
Table of abbreviations appears when needed
Appendix is optional
```

## Required Structure

```text
Title page
Declaration / academic ethics statement
Abstract
Acknowledgements
Table of Contents
Introduction
Chapter One - Literature Review I
Chapter Two - Literature Review II
Chapter Three - Methodology
Chapter Four - Findings / Analysis / Discussion
  4.1 Findings
  4.2 Analysis
  4.3 Discussion
Concluding Remarks
Bibliography
Appendix
Academic Ethics Form
```

## Word Allocation

```text
Abstract: around 200 words
Introduction: around 1,000 words
Chapter One - Literature Review I: around 3,000 words
Chapter Two - Literature Review II: around 3,000 words
Chapter Three - Methodology: around 2,000 words
Chapter Four - Findings / Analysis / Discussion: around 5,000 words
Concluding Remarks: around 1,000 words
```

## Academic Writing Rules

```text
- write at Masters level
- use critical analysis, not description only
- avoid first person in the main dissertation
- use "this study" or "the author" where self-reference is needed
- write mainly in past tense
- proofread for grammar, spelling, punctuation, and clarity
- maintain a clear argument from research problem to conclusion
```

## Literature Review Rules

```text
- organize by themes, trends, theories, and controversies
- connect directly to research questions
- synthesize what is known and not known
- evaluate strengths and weaknesses of sources
- include recent and seminal literature
- avoid a source-by-source shopping list
- use theory as an analytical tool
```

## Methodology Rules

```text
- explain and justify the methodology
- explain why alternatives were rejected where relevant
- discuss data collection methods
- discuss data analysis methods
- distinguish quantitative and qualitative analysis where relevant
- explain research practicalities
- identify limitations
- make the method clear enough to judge reliability and validity
```

## Findings, Analysis, Discussion Rules

```text
4.1 Findings: present research data, results, and findings
4.2 Analysis: interpret the findings in the author's own words
4.3 Discussion: connect primary research, secondary literature, theory, and the author's argument
```

## Referencing Rules

```text
- use UCA Harvard referencing
- do not mix referencing systems
- every citation must match a bibliography entry
- bibliography must be alphabetical
- bibliography appears before appendices
- direct quotations require page numbers where available
- paraphrased ideas still require citations
- secondary references use "cited in" where unavoidable
```

## Image Referencing Rules

```text
- each image uses a figure number
- caption appears directly beneath the image
- figure numbers follow order of appearance
- list of illustrations is ordered by figure number
- image references must match captions
- list of illustrations is excluded from word count
```
