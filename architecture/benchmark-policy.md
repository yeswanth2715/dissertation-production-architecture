# Dissertation Benchmark Policy

Benchmarks convert the UCA Level 7 mark descriptors into measurable quality targets. They do not guarantee a grade, but they set the target standard for generation and review.

## Target Grade Standard

Default target:

```text
High distinction target: 80-89
Stretch target: 90-100
Minimum acceptable final target: 70-79
```

The architecture should aim for the 80-89 band unless the user sets a different target. A submission should not be treated as final-ready if any core benchmark is below 70-level quality.



## 80-90% Level 7 Quality Benchmark

The 80-90% target is a quality standard for the whole dissertation, not only for writing style. A project may be called high-distinction target quality only when the evidence supports strong performance across all core dimensions.

### Scored Dimensions

Score each dimension from 0 to 100 using the project benchmark scorecard.

Academic writing and argument: 20%
- precise, coherent and controlled MSc-level prose
- clear argument from research problem to conclusion
- mainly past-tense academic reporting
- no drafting notes, meta-language, generic claims or unnecessary repetition
- terminology is consistent and paragraphs develop an analytical point

Critical literature analysis: 20%
- current and seminal sources are synthesised by themes and debates
- theories are used as analytical tools, not described mechanically
- strengths, weaknesses, disagreements and research gaps are evaluated
- material claims are supported by appropriate sources

Methodology and research design: 15%
- method directly answers the research questions
- sampling, instrument, data collection and analysis are explicit
- alternatives and limitations are justified
- reliability, validity, ethics and practical constraints are addressed
- the method is reproducible from the dissertation and project evidence

Findings, analysis and discussion: 25%
- findings are reported from actual data or traceable sources
- every statistic, table and figure is verified
- analysis explains meaning rather than repeating results
- discussion connects evidence to theory, literature and research questions
- recommendations are proportionate to the evidence
- causality, transferability and generalisation are not overstated

Referencing, originality and evidence integrity: 10%
- citations and bibliography are consistent and Harvard compliant
- sources are credible, relevant and sufficiently current
- no fabricated citations, data, participants, quotations or images
- survey denominators, exclusions, coding rules and calculations are auditable
- topic-specific writing is independent from other projects

Professional execution and compliance: 10%
- approved template is preserved
- chapter, heading, table and figure numbering is correct
- word-count allocation follows the active guideline
- figures are relevant, source-backed or data-derived
- placeholders, internal workflow language and formatting defects are removed
- final DOCX passes structural and render QA

### High-Distinction Gate

80-89 target:
- weighted score is 80-89
- every core dimension is at least 75
- writing, critical analysis and findings/discussion are each at least 80
- no unresolved Critical issue
- no unsupported major conclusion
- all required evidence and QA artifacts are present

90-100 stretch:
- weighted score is at least 90
- every core dimension is at least 85
- the dissertation demonstrates exceptional originality, synthesis and judgement
- all claims remain proportionate to the evidence

70-79 distinction-ready:
- weighted score is 70-79
- no Critical issue
- any dimension below 75 is recorded as a revision priority

A benchmark score is an internal quality estimate, not a guaranteed university mark. The final grade remains the assessor's decision.

### Evidence Required For Scoring

Each scorecard entry must include:
- evidence location in the project
- check performed
- result
- unresolved risk, if any
- reviewer confidence

Model judgement may interpret quality, but deterministic scripts must verify counts, headings, tables, figures, citations, denominators, placeholders and file structure before a score is assigned.

## Benchmark Dimensions

Use six benchmark dimensions:

```text
1. Knowledge and context
2. Literature synthesis and criticality
3. Methodology and research design
4. Findings, analysis, and discussion
5. Professional academic execution
6. Compliance, originality, and referencing
```

## Grade Bands

### 90-100: Exceptional

```text
- original and exceptional critical understanding
- sophisticated synthesis of theory, context, and methods
- research problem is highly complex and handled with strong judgment
- findings and discussion produce clear original insight
- professional execution is near submission-perfect
```

### 80-89: High Distinction Target

```text
- excellent and highly sophisticated critical understanding
- strong use of current and relevant literature
- methodology is clearly justified and alternatives are considered
- analysis connects findings, theory, and research questions convincingly
- argument is coherent from introduction to conclusion
- citations, formatting, and academic voice are consistently controlled
```

### 70-79: Distinction Ready

```text
- comprehensive critical understanding
- literature review is organized by themes and debates
- research design is defensible
- findings are analysed, not merely described
- discussion connects primary and secondary research
- structure and referencing are mostly clean
```

### 60-69: Merit Level

```text
- significant understanding but less sophisticated synthesis
- literature coverage is relevant but may be uneven
- methodology is generally appropriate but under-justified
- analysis exists but may not fully connect evidence and theory
- structure is acceptable with some weak transitions
```

### Below 60: Not Final Ready

```text
- descriptive rather than critical
- weak or unsupported research problem
- source use is thin, outdated, or poorly connected
- methodology does not persuasively answer the research question
- findings are reported without enough analysis
- referencing, structure, or originality risks remain unresolved
```

## Final-Ready Thresholds

Before final dissertation output, target these minimums:

```text
Knowledge and context: 80 target, 70 minimum
Literature synthesis and criticality: 80 target, 70 minimum
Methodology and research design: 80 target, 70 minimum
Findings, analysis, and discussion: 80 target, 70 minimum
Professional academic execution: 80 target, 70 minimum
Compliance, originality, and referencing: pass required; no unresolved critical issue
```

## Survey Readiness Benchmark

Before generating a survey questionnaire, the project must show:

```text
- Introduction, Chapter One, Chapter Two, and Chapter Three exist in draft form
- research aim, objectives, and questions are clear
- survey design is justified by the methodology
- target participants and sampling logic are defined
- questionnaire items map to research objectives
- ethics and consent language are included where needed
```

## Benchmark Usage

```text
Draft stage: use benchmark notes to guide revision.
Professor review stage: map feedback to benchmark dimensions.
Final stage: complete benchmark-scorecard.md before final DOCX generation.
```
