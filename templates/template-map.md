# Template Map

Templates are output shells. They are separate from guidelines because they control document shape rather than assessment rules.

## Current Template Source

```text
templates/source/<dissertation-template>.docx
```

## Template-Derived Output Order

```text
Dissertation Title
Master title
Name
Year
Abstract
Contents
Acknowledgements
Statement of compliance with academic ethics and avoidance of plagiarism
Dissertation Thesis blank page
Introduction
Chapter One - Literature Review I
Chapter Two - Literature Review II
Chapter Three - Methodology
Chapter Four - Findings / Analysis / Discussion
  4.1 Findings
  4.2 Analysis
  4.3 Discussion
Concluding Remarks
Bibliography
Appendix
Academic Ethics Form
```

## Why This Is Not A Guideline

The template does not decide academic quality by itself. It provides the physical structure for final outputs.

Guidelines answer:

```text
What rules must the dissertation obey?
```

Templates answer:

```text
What should the output artifact look like?
```
