# Fast QA Checklist

## Structure

- [ ] Project discovery has identified available inputs, missing inputs, research type, and required outputs.
- [ ] Required dissertation sections are present.
- [ ] Chapter order matches BSBI/UCA structure.
- [ ] Each chapter starts on a new page in final DOCX planning.
- [ ] Chapter Four includes 4.1 Findings, 4.2 Analysis, and 4.3 Discussion.

## Headings

- [ ] Subheadings are context-based.
- [ ] Chapter One subheadings use 1.x.
- [ ] Chapter Two subheadings use 2.x.
- [ ] Chapter Three subheadings use 3.x.
- [ ] Chapter Four subheadings use 4.x.

## Referencing

- [ ] Harvard citations are consistent.
- [ ] Citations match bibliography entries.
- [ ] Bibliography is alphabetical.
- [ ] Direct quotes include page numbers where available.

## Originality

- [ ] No prior-topic prose is reused.
- [ ] No close paraphrasing is visible.
- [ ] Material claims map to sources/findings.
- [ ] Images are source-based, not AI-generated.
- [ ] Figures follow `architecture/visual-style-policy.md`.

## Output Readiness

- [ ] Benchmark scorecard has been updated.
- [ ] Introduction exists before survey questionnaire generation.
- [ ] Chapter One - Literature Review I exists before survey questionnaire generation.
- [ ] Chapter Two - Literature Review II exists before survey questionnaire generation.
- [ ] Chapter Three - Methodology exists before survey questionnaire generation.
- [ ] Survey questionnaire was generated only after pre-survey package approval.
- [ ] Returned survey results exist before findings are written for survey-based projects.
- [ ] Word-count targets are in range.
- [ ] Professor feedback is addressed.
- [ ] Placeholders are removed from final-facing outputs.
- [ ] Render QA is scheduled if final DOCX is approved.
