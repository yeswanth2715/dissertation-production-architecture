# Survey Questionnaire Template

Use this structure when generating survey questionnaire outputs.

```text
# Survey Questionnaire

Dissertation topic:
Research aim:
Target participants:
Estimated completion time:
Ethics note:

## Participant Information

## Consent Statement

## Section A: Participant Profile

## Section B: Topic-Specific Questions

## Section C: Experience / Perception Questions

## Section D: Open-Ended Questions

## Closing Statement
```

Questionnaire rules:

```text
- questions must map to research objectives
- avoid leading questions
- use consistent Likert scales where relevant
- include demographic questions only when justified
- include ethics and consent language when required
- keep wording simple and neutral
```

Output formats:

```text
survey-questionnaire.md
survey-questionnaire.docx
```
